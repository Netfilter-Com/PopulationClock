Privacy Policy — Population Clock

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Population Clock does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

  • The app does not require account creation or login.
  • The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind.
  • The app does not access your location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.
  • All population, demographic, and economic data displayed in the app is bundled with the app itself and does not require an internet connection to view.
  • In-app purchases, where offered, are processed entirely by Apple's App Store; we do not receive or store any payment information.

Because Population Clock collects no data, there is no data to share with third parties, and no data retention or deletion process is necessary.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at suporte@netfilter.com.br.
