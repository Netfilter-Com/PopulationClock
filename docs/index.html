Population Clock — Support

Population Clock shows a live estimate of the world population and of each country's, along with births, deaths, growth and other demographic and economic indicators.

Contact

For questions, bug reports or feedback, email us at suporte@netfilter.com.br. Please include your device model and iOS version so we can help faster.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the data come from?
Population and economic figures are based on World Bank indicators and are bundled with the app. The live counter is a simulation extrapolated from those figures, so it is an estimate rather than an exact count.

Does the app need an internet connection?
No. All data is bundled with the app.

Which devices are supported?
iPhone and iPad running iOS 15 or later.
